# Antura Project Update: Field-Test Feedback and Next Languages

In its January 2018 newsletter, VGWB reported positive literacy and psychosocial feedback from the 2017 field test in Jordan, while noting that the game still needed improvement. A new version was planned for February, alongside publication of the field-test report.

The update also described plans to make Antura adaptable to more languages and contexts, subject to securing funding and partners.

Source: [VGWB Newsletter, January 2018](https://vgwb.org/2018/02/newsletter-january-2018/).
