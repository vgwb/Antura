# Fourth Consortium Meeting in Wrocław

The ANTURA consortium met at the Dolnośląska Szkoła Wyższa campus in Wrocław on 8-9 September 2025 to review the game and prepare for the large-scale pilot in France and Poland.

Partners discussed technical development, cultural content, pilot planning and project milestones. They also visited the Leonardo School in Wrocław, where the Polish pilot was planned to take place.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
