# Antura Presented at a Language-Teaching Seminar

On 11 February 2026, Lucie Durand of Académie de Nantes represented the ANTURA project at a seminar for language teachers and trainers.

Her presentation introduced the project's aim of helping children and families learn the language of their host country and understand its culture. It also addressed inclusion, differentiated instruction and the potential role of digital tools in language teaching.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
