# Antura Project Update: Launch, Downloads and Field Testing

In its April 2017 newsletter, VGWB reported that Antura and the Letters had launched for free on Android and iOS and had been announced as one of two EduApp4Syria winners. The game had passed 10,000 downloads, and field testing was underway privately in Morocco, Turkey and Lebanon, with an official test in Jordan about to begin.

The team also described plans to continue testing and improving the game through the end of 2017 and to publish its code as open source.

Source: [VGWB Newsletter, April 2017](https://vgwb.org/2017/05/newsletter-april-2017/).
