# Antura and the Letters 1.2.0: Arabic Release

Released in February 2018, version 1.2.0 was the first full Arabic release. It introduced a new Arabic curriculum and map, Antura's room and shop, photo saving, daily rewards and notifications, and feminine Arabic localizations and speech.
