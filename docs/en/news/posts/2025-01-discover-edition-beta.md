# Learn with Antura 2025 Beta: Discover Edition

The 2025 beta introduced the Discover Edition, adding a cultural exploration module to Learn with Antura.

## What's included

- Initial Discover quests for France and Poland, with topics and knowledge cards
- Classroom mode
- A new cat character
- An updated website with content generated from the game
- Unity 6, a localization package, and editor tools for managing and validating content

The release also removed the Facebook SDK and began a broader refactoring of content management.
