# Antura Presented at the International Forum on ICT and Education 2030

VGWB co-founder Francesco Cavallari presented Antura at UNESCO's International Forum on ICT and Education 2030 in Qingdao, China. At the time, the Arabic version had been tested in Jordan, and VGWB was seeking partners to develop additional language versions.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2017/10/being-part-of-the-solution-education-2030/).
