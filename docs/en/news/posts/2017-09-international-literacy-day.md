# Antura and International Literacy Day 2017

For International Literacy Day, VGWB highlighted Antura and the Letters as a digital tool supporting Arabic reading and writing for Syrian children. The article connected the project to broader efforts to use digital learning to advance literacy.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2017/09/international-literacy-day-2017/).
