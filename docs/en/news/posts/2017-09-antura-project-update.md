# Antura Project Update: Downloads, Testing and Open Source

In its August 2017 newsletter, VGWB reported that Antura and the Letters was free on Android and iOS and had passed 15,000 downloads. The newsletter also shared early user ratings, noted that the team was investigating retention, and described field testing focused on literacy and well-being.

The project had been made fully open source, with its Arabic repository published on GitHub. VGWB was also exploring partners and funding for additional language versions.

Source: [VGWB Newsletter, August 2017](https://vgwb.org/2017/09/newsletter-august-2017/).
