# Antura Selected as an EduApp4Syria Finalist

Antura and the Letters was selected as one of three finalists in the EduApp4Syria competition. The selection brought funding to continue development of the literacy game for Syrian children.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2016/09/antura-among-the-3-eduapp4syria-finalists/).
