# Antura Receives Spanish Red Cross Technology Award

Antura and the Letters received a Spanish Red Cross award recognizing the humanitarian use of technology. The award was announced in connection with Barcelona's 4YFN event.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2019/02/antura-award-spanish-red-cross/).
