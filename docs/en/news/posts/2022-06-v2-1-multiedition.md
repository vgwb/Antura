# Antura and the Letters 2.1.0: The Multiedition One App

Released in June 2022, version 2.1.0 brought multiple learning editions into one app. Supported interface languages included Arabic, Dari Persian, English, French, Ukrainian and Russian. Learning content included courses for reading Arabic and Dari Persian and for learning Polish, English and French.

The release also introduced online analytics and local notifications, alongside bug fixes.
