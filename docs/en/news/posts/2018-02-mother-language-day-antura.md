# Antura and International Mother Language Day

For International Mother Language Day, VGWB highlighted the value of multilingual education and described Antura and the Letters as a way for Syrian refugee children to learn to read in Arabic, their mother language. The article also invited partners to help extend the project to more languages.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2018/02/21f-international-mother-language-day/).
