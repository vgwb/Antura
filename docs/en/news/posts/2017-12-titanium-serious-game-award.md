# Antura Wins Titanium Serious Game of the Year

Antura and the Letters received the Titanium prize for Serious Game of the Year at the Fun & Serious Game Festival in Bilbao. VGWB shared the recognition with the partners and contributors who helped create the literacy game.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2017/12/we-won-the-titanium-serious-game-of-the-year/).
