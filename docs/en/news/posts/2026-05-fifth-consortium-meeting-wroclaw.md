# Fifth Consortium Meeting in Wrocław

The ANTURA consortium met in Wrocław, Poland, on 26-27 May 2026 to review progress and coordinate the project's final implementation phase.

Partners discussed findings from the large-scale testing in France and Poland, game improvements, project milestones and preparations for teacher and mediator training. They also reviewed dissemination plans and agreed on next steps.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
