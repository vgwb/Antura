# Teacher Capacity-Building Trainings Begin in France and Poland

ANTURA's teacher capacity-building phase began in April 2026, with training activities in France and Poland for teachers, educators and school mediators working with migrant and refugee children.

The programme introduces the game alongside a classroom methodology. Its goals include training at least 610 teachers in France and Poland, 40 mediators in France, and 150 additional teachers across Europe. Sessions also address second-language learning through children's first languages, intercultural exchange and family involvement.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
