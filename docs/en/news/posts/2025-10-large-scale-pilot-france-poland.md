# Large-Scale Pilot in France and Poland

The ANTURA version 3 school pilot ran from October 2025 to April 2026, with migrant and refugee children in France and Poland exploring the game individually at school and at home.

The evaluation plan considered second-language learning, cultural knowledge, user experience and psychosocial well-being. The project news page describes expected participation of 70-80 children in France and around 120 in Poland; it does not report final participation or results. Findings were intended to inform version 4 and the project's impact evaluation.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
