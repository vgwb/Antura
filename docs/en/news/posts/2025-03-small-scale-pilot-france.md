# Small-Scale Pilot Begins in France

The ANTURA small-scale pilot began in March 2025 with Académie de Nantes in France. Teachers and children tested the game, and their feedback was intended to inform further development and classroom use.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
