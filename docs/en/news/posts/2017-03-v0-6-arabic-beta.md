# Antura and the Letters 0.6.0: Arabic Beta

Released in March 2017, version 0.6.0 was the first Arabic beta of Antura and the Letters. The public launch on March 21 coincided with the announcement that Antura had won the EduApp4Syria competition; the full Arabic release followed in 2018.

Source: [Antura and the Letters is out!](https://vgwb.org/2017/03/antura-and-the-letters-is-out/).
