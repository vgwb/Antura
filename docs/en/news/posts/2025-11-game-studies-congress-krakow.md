# Antura at the Game Studies Congress in Kraków

On 13-14 November 2025, researchers from the University of Lower Silesia presented Antura at the National Congress of Gaming Studies in Kraków, Poland.

They led a quest-design workshop introducing the game and its creation process. Participants then worked on their own quest ideas, exploring how games can support refugee children.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
