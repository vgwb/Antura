# When We Play, We Learn

This story from the launch period describes children playing Antura and the Letters as a way to learn Arabic. It highlights the project's goal of making literacy practice engaging for Syrian children and acknowledges the collaboration with Cologne Game Lab, Wixel Studios and NORAD's EduApp4Syria competition.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2017/03/when-we-play-we-learn/).
