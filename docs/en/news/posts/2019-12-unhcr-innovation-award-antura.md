# VGWB Wins the UNHCR NGO Innovation Award

Video Games Without Borders was selected as one of three winners of the UN Refugee Agency's NGO Innovation Award. The article connected the recognition to Antura and the Letters, noting its literacy work with refugee children and plans to expand to Afghanistan.

The award ceremony took place at the Global Refugee Forum in Geneva on 16 December 2019.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2019/12/unhcr-award/).
