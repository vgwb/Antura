# Antura Nominated for Two Games for Change Awards

Antura and the Letters was selected as a finalist in two categories at the 2018 Games for Change Awards: Best Learning Game and Most Significant Impact. The nomination followed its earlier EduApp4Syria and Titanium recognitions.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2018/06/antura-at-games-for-change-2018-awards/).
