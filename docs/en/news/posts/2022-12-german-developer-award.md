# Antura Wins the German Developer Award for Social Commitment

Antura and the Letters won the Special Award for Social Commitment at the 2022 German Developer Awards, presented by Gaming-Aid e.V. The award recognized the project's work in literacy, language learning and psychosocial support for refugee children.

Source: [VGWB News](https://vgwb.org/2022/12/german-developer-award-2022-for-antura-the-letters/).
