# Third Consortium Meeting in Málaga

The ANTURA consortium met at the Social Innovation Centre La Noria in Málaga, Spain, on 4-5 November 2024. The first day included teacher training for partners involved in the French and Polish pilots; the second focused on project progress, milestones and next steps.

Source: [ANTURA project news](https://www.anturaproject.eu/news).
